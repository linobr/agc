QUICK GUIDE

From scan to test.

  1. Import a local GLB. It is parsed in your browser and never uploaded.
  2. Align it with Move, Rotate, Scale, Ground and the numeric inspector.
  3. Inspect its simplified collider overlay. Use a box for a quick test.
  4. Test physics, grab an object, release it, and reset to your edited scene.

Scan colliders are approximations. Walk Test detects upward-facing triangles, with limited spawn clearance checks; it cannot reconstruct every scan.
